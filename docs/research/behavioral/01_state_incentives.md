## gov-stablecoin-1 — GENIUS Act channels speculation into T-bill demand [C] [FLAGSHIP — AMENDED]

**Amended claim (verdict text, use this):** The GENIUS Act (S.1582, 119th
Congress, signed 2025-07-18) channels crypto-speculation demand toward
short-dated US-debt demand in practice, though not by strict statutory
compulsion: dollar stablecoin issuers must back coins 1:1 from a statutory
MENU of permitted reserves — US coins/currency, insured deposits, Treasury
bills of <=93-day remaining maturity, T-bill-backed repos/reverse repos,
government money market funds, central bank reserves (CRS IN12553; S.1582
sec. 4) — with short-dated Treasuries the dominant category in issuer
practice, not a legal mandate. The Brookings Hutchins Center / Aspen Economic
Strategy Group study projects NET NEW T-bill demand of $400B-$2.3T by 2030 as
a private-forecaster scenario range whose realized value depends on
substitution: flows sourced from money market funds or bank deposits largely
reallocate existing T-bill demand (Brookings 2025; Kansas City Fed 2025; BIS
WP 1219), while flows from offshore dollar-savings demand are closer to net
new. Sponsor Sen. Hagerty stated on the record he wrote it in part to
solidify dollar dominance and create structural Treasury demand
(Benzinga/Yahoo Finance, 2025).

**Verdict: AMENDED — direction CONFIRMED, statutory wording REFUTED.** The
original claim's "must hold reserves in Treasuries of <=93-day maturity" is
not the statute (Treasuries are one option on the permitted menu; "forced
US-debt demand" is issuer practice, not compulsion). The $400B-$2.3T range is
confirmed as the Brookings/Aspen scenario range but must be tagged
net-of-substitution-assumptions, not first-round arithmetic (Kansas City Fed
2025; BIS WP 1219 show gross overstates net when funded from MMFs/deposits).
Hagerty attribution confirmed. Seigniorage-offset caveat confirmed. Referee
did not read the full statutory text end-to-end (relied on CRS summary).

**Citations:** Congress.gov S.1582 text; CRS IN12553
(congress.gov/crs_external_products/IN/PDF/IN12553/IN12553.1.pdf); Brookings,
"Stablecoins after GENIUS" (brookings.edu/articles/stablecoins-after-genius/);
Kansas City Fed 2025, "Stablecoins Could Increase Treasury Demand, but Only by
Reducing Demand for Other Assets"; BIS WP 1219 (bis.org/publ/work1219.pdf);
Yahoo Finance/Benzinga on Hagerty
(finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/genius-act-could-create-2-140533220.html).

**Mechanism:** Retail speculators park trading float in stablecoins -> issuers
are compelled-in-practice buyers of short-dated Treasuries -> state borrowing
cost falls at the margin, dollar network effects extend offshore. The state
profits from the SIZE of the speculative float, not its P&L — permitting
maximal retail churn is directly fiscally rational.

**Testable:** NOT with current data (no stablecoin issuance/reserve feed).
Weak proxy: USDT/USD basis on Kraken feed as stress indicator only.

## gov-offshore-1 — "let it burn offshore" asymmetry [C] [FLAGSHIP — AMENDED]

**Amended claim (verdict text, use this):** The 'let it burn offshore'
asymmetry was enacted policy through ~2024 but is ATTENUATING, not stable:
FCA PS20/10 (in force 2021-01-06) banned retail crypto derivatives AND crypto
ETNs onshore with an FCA-estimated consumer saving of around GBP 53m (FCA
press release states no periodicity; figure covers both product classes) —
the cETN half was reversed effective 2025-10-08 for RIE-traded notes while
the derivatives ban remains; the 2023-11-21 DOJ/FinCEN Binance resolution
($4.3B total, FinCEN $3.4B civil penalty, largest in Treasury history)
required Binance.com/Binance Holdings' complete exit from the US while the
venue kept operating globally and the separate registered MSB Binance.US
continued domestically; under MiCA (CASP transition outer limit 2026-07-01,
several member states earlier) EU users pushed off unlicensed spot venues
still reach unlicensed 50x perp venues one click away. HOWEVER, the US
onshored retail perp leverage in 2025: CFTC-regulated perpetual-style futures
launched for US retail 2025-07-21 via Coinbase Financial Markets (up to 10x
intraday leverage; Kraken Pro likewise), explicitly to repatriate offshore
perp volume. The exported cascade engine is now leverage ABOVE ~10x,
concentrated on offshore venues our feeds treat as read-only — the asymmetry
survives in the EU and at the high-leverage tail, not as blanket US policy.

**Verdict: AMENDED — pillars CONFIRMED, present-tense synthesis over-reached.**
(1) US regime reversal: onshore <=10x perps mean "leverage is exported" is
false for the US except the 50-125x tail. (2) FCA partially reopened (cETNs
2025-10-08); the GBP 53m estimate covered derivatives AND ETNs with no stated
per-year periodicity, so "GBP 53m/yr for the derivatives ban" was unsupported
as cited. (3) "Complete exit" binds Binance.com/Binance Holdings;
Binance.US (BAM Trading) continued. ATTR-2 testability unaffected —
arguably sharpened: high-leverage tail makes offshore-cascade-precedes-
Kraken-dislocation the crisper hypothesis. Unresolved: FCA PS20/10 CBA annex
periodicity.

**Citations:** FCA PS20/10 press release 2020-10-06 (fca.org.uk); FCA retail
cETN reopening (fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-opens-retail-access-crypto-etns);
US Treasury JY1925 2023-11-21; FinCEN largest-settlement release
(fincen.gov); DOJ, US v. Binance Holdings; MiCA Art. 143(3) + ESMA statement
ESMA75-113276571-1679; Coinbase perps launch
(coinbase.com/blog/perpetual-futures-have-arrived-in-the-us); CoinDesk opinion
2026-07-01 on the offshore leverage window.

**Mechanism:** Onshore regulators internalize the political cost of visible
domestic retail blowups but not offshore ones; the cascade engine (high
leverage) concentrates on venues outside their books while onshore taxable
spot/ETF flow is kept. State-layer complement to who-loses-to-us: the
forced-flow pool is structurally concentrated on exactly the venues our feeds
treat as read-only.

**Testable:** Partial — names our gap: ATTR-2 (docs/HANDOFF.md lines 97-116)
records zero liquidation/OI awareness. OKX swap OI + liquidation feeds would
test whether offshore cascade events precede Kraken spot dislocations
exceeding the 43-118 bps bar. See 06 spec, OWED DATA.

## gov-outcomes-1 — states permit despite measured 73-81% retail loss rates [C]

**Claim:** States permit retail crypto access despite regulator-grade
evidence that 73-81% of retail app investors lost money on bitcoin (BIS WP
1049: 95 countries, 2015-2022; new users disproportionately young and male;
on-chain data shows largest holders selling into small-user buying).

**Citations:** Auer, Cornelli, Doerr, Frost & Gambacorta, BIS WP 1049, Nov
2022 (bis.org/publ/work1049.htm) *(citation corrected 2026-08-27: page
previously dated the WP 2023; the working paper is November 2022 — 2023
belongs to BIS Bulletin 69 and the SSRN revision 4357559; the 73-81% loss
estimate, 95 countries 2015-2022, young/male skew, and whales-selling-into-
retail confirmed verbatim)*; BIS Bulletin 69, "Crypto shocks and retail
losses", 2023 (bis.org/publ/bisbull69.htm).

**Mechanism:** Retail losses are diffuse, private, self-attributed
(who-loses-to-us: pool "replenished, not educated"); permitting's benefits
(tax on flow, lobbying money, Treasury demand) are concentrated and legible.
Inclusion delivered is inclusion in a negative-sum flow tax. BIS is
central-bank empirical, not journal-peer-reviewed — hence C.

**Testable:** Analog on our corpus: WHY-1 replicates the shape internally —
91% of era-4 accrual (probes n=49, net -1.05%/trip) is tuition below
round-trip at true 40/80 bps (docs/HANDOFF.md lines 115-116).

unverified until checked against OpenSecrets directly.

Crypto, Big Spending" 2024; FactCheck.org "Fairshake" 2026-08.

**Mechanism:** Exchanges monetize retail flow via fees; a slice of fee
revenue purchases the regulatory permission that keeps the flow legal — the

scope (corporate-only vs PAC-triad vs Fairshake-lifetime); do not average.

**Testable:** No (no political-finance feed; out of scope). Context for

## gov-tax-1 — India permit-and-tax: flow levy above any behavioral edge [C]

**Claim:** India collected Rs 1,095.80 crore (~USD 130M) in crypto TDS
across FY23-FY25 (Rs 221.27 / 362.70 / 511.83 crore; +41% YoY in FY25) from
a 1% transaction-level TDS plus a flat 30% gains tax with no loss offset.

**Citations:** Finance Ministry reply to Parliament, Dec 2025 (as reported
by Business Today story 505820 and hokanews) — carries all three fiscal-year
figures and the FY25 +41%. *(Provenance corrected 2026-08-27: page
previously cited "Business Standard (govt reply to Parliament), 2024-02;
MEXC blog aggregating FY25 official figures, 2025" — a 2024-02 article
cannot source the FY25 figure; the numbers themselves are confirmed
verbatim.)*

**Mechanism:** The 1% TDS taxes GROSS flow regardless of trader P&L — the
state is an exchange-fee-like levy on the same retail churn our fee-reality
work prices. Scale check: this 3-year national take (~$130M) is ~33x SMALLER
than the single Binance US resolution ($4.3B) — episodic penalty extraction,
not trading tax, is the larger direct state revenue channel to date.

**Testable:** Analog only: a ~100 bps flow levy sits above our entire
43-118 bps distinguishable-edge bar
(docs/quant/2026-08-22_walkforward_resampling_tranche2.md). No India feed.

## gov-mica-1 — EU licensing over prohibition, leaky at the leverage margin [C]

**Claim:** MiCA imposes a full CASP authorization regime (transition ends
2026-07-01, unauthorized firms wind down) with substantial compliance cost on
onshore providers, while offshore platforms serve EU users "for a fraction of
the costs" — enforcement economics of prohibition rejected in favor of a
fee-and-oversight perimeter that leaks by design at the leverage margin.

**Citations:** Sumsub MiCA overview 2026; Finextra "MiCA vs UK regulation";
CoinDesk opinion 2026-07-01.

**Mechanism:** Licensing converts an unenforceable prohibition (criminology
lens DAT prediction: socially-transmitted manipulation persists regardless of
single-venue countermeasures) into supervisory fees, AML visibility, and a
taxable domestic industry — while the highest-loss product (offshore perps)
stays reachable, feeding the gov-offshore-1 cascade engine. Grade C: texts
primary, cost-asymmetry quantification journalistic, no prohibition-cost
counterfactual published anywhere.

**Testable:** Not with current data. Downstream cascade prediction shares the
gov-offshore-1 instrument (ATTR-2 liquidation/OI) — one feed tests both.
