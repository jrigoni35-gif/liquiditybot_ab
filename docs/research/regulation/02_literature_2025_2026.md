## Synthesis

Margin: with no established edge, leverage adds only quadratic variance drag
(row 10), and 2026 evidence (row 4) shows it converts a signal failure into a
survival event rather than fixing it. The 10x cap is inert today (never
bound); keep it unexercised until an edge clears the overfit battery.

Signed flow / reversal: the direction is independently confirmed (row 1), but
so is the economics - a real reversal that does not clear even a 5 bp cost.
Treat it as a candidate feature only, through the full overfit battery, and
never as a reason to use margin.
