Net Payoff Function:The net value V of entering a Brownian-driven game or contract with an entry fee C₀ and a stopping rule (exit condition) at time τ is given by:\[V=\mathbb{E}[e^{-r\tau }\text{Payoff}(X_{\tau })]-C_{0}\]where r is the risk-free discount rate and \[X_{\tau }\] is the value of the underlying asset or wealth process at the exit time τ. Fair Game / Zero-Profit Condition:In competitive entry-fee models (such as speculative auctions or risk-taking contests), the entry fee adjusts the equilibrium behavior. Rational agents will only pay the entry fee C₀ if the expected discounted future payoff matches or exceeds that cost, leading to an optimal boundary problem solvable via stochastic calculus and Itô's lemma. Ruin and Absorption Probabilities:If the entry fee depletes initial capital, the process starts at X₀ = Initial Endowment - C₀. The probability of hitting a lower absorbing barrier (bankruptcy/ruin) before an upper target barrier increases because of the reduced starting buffer.
