### 1. EXECUTION SUCCESS IS NOT EVIDENCE OF ANALYTICAL CORRECTNESS. LLM agents produce runnable analysis code far more often than correct analysis: GPT-4o emitted a non-empty executable analysis 96% of the time (most large LMs >60%) while the best agreement with expert ground-truth analytical decisions was F1 44.8 (95% CI 43.0-46.3, GPT-4o + ReAct) and 43.9 (42.6-44.9, Claude 3.5 Sonnet one-turn); independently, the best public system at DS-1000's publication (Codex-002) solved 43.3% of 1,000 realistic data-science problems across seven Python libraries. An LLM-written measurement instrument that runs clean is un-validated.

### 3. UNDER-TESTING DOES NOT MERELY INFLATE SCORES UNIFORMLY — IT REVERSES RANKINGS. ## Open questions carried forward

- What is the published rate of SILENT numeric-defect classes in LLM-written analysis code — wrong denominator, look-ahead or off-by-one window, leakage, wrong group-by, unit or reference-frame mismatch — as distinct from wrong answers a test suite catches? No located benchmark separates crash / test-caught-wrong / silently-plausible-wrong, and every attempt in this run to convert a test-detected rate into a silent-error rate failed verification. If that base rate does not exist in the literature, the absence is itself the finding and should be reported as such rather than proxied.

- Is there ANY measured effect size for adversarial cross-checking by a SECOND model versus self-verification by the same model, at the level of analysis or measurement CODE rather than reasoning traces? The candidate source (arXiv 2606.05976, including its 0% baseline self-correction cells and its +23-to-93pp attribution effect) failed verification here. The bot's own case (iii) is a single uncontrolled instance in favor; EvalPlus's second re-implementation is the strongest published analogue but is human-written, not model-written.

- Does any published work measure SAME-CHANNEL, SAME-PRINCIPAL specific-versus-general instruction conflict — the exact shape of live case (v), where a general prohibition and a violating specific instruction sat in one prompt from one principal? IH-Benchmark measures cross-level hierarchy (system>user, user>tool) and explicitly scopes itself to bounded single-task scenarios, so the failure mode that actually wrote rows into a production hash-chained audit ledger is currently UNMEASURED by anything located.

- All of angle 5 remains open and is the largest unaddressed block: what do SR 11-7 and OCC 2011-12 require, in their own section text, for models used ONLY for measurement rather than for pricing or capital; what is the exact documented mechanism and magnitude of the JPMorgan CIO 2012 VaR model change; and what is the published base rate for the fraction of researched strategies a professional desk KILLS on an 'edge indistinguishable from zero' readout versus extending the sample or changing the target (magnitude/expectancy, cost-aware labels, meta-labeling)? This is the block that would convert the bot's measurement result into a defensible capital decision, and none of it was scanned.
