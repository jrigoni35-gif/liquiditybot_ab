# Research session 2026-07-18 — peer-reviewed foundations, argued

Six lanes swept (deep-research harness: parallel search → fetch → 3-vote
adversarial verify; area-1 claims verified 3-0, later lanes synthesized
from fetched primary-source extracts). For each: canon, the ONE mechanism

## 1. Inventory management (ADOPTED → sizer `inventory_skew`, shadow)

- Avellaneda & Stoikov 2008; Guéant–Lehalle–Fernandez-Tapia 2013
  (Math. Financ. Econ.); Guéant 2016 (arXiv:1605.01862); Ho–Stoll 1981.
- Verified 3-0: reservation price r = s − q·γ·σ²(T−t) (skew center
  against inventory); spread width separates from center; hard bound ±Q
  stops inventory-increasing quotes while exits stay allowed (exactly
  our invariant-5 philosophy, independently derived); A-S Table 1:
  inventory skew costs ~6% expected profit for >2× lower P&L variance.
- Adopted: signed, direction-aware entry scaling
  `1 − γ·(q·d)·(σ/σ_ref)²` — floor-bounded, reduction-exempt, SHADOW
  mode default. Distinct from the existing unsigned inventory_aggression.
- Failure mode honored: closed forms degrade near risk limits (hard cap
  still governs); RL-tuned γ (Alpha-AS) showed extreme-drawdown tails —
  γ stays a config knob under the gated tuning pass, never learned live.

## 2. Adverse selection (markouts over VPIN)

- Glosten–Milgrom 1985; Easley–López de Prado–O'Hara 2011/2012 (VPIN);
  Andersen–Bondarenko 2014 (VPIN refutation: no early warning pre flash
  crash, predictive content is mechanical volume artifact); ELO
  rejoinder; crypto: adverse-selection ~10% of effective spread on
  Bitfinex, VPIN predicts BTC jumps (VAR evidence) with session/day
  effects.
- Verdict: VPIN is CONTESTED in its home market — do not build on it.
  Markouts are the uncontested practitioner standard (mid-to-mid at
  multiple horizons) and we already record them (post-fill mark-out
  feature). Next mechanism (deferred, designed): per-asset rolling
  markout-toxicity monitor — sustained negative fill markouts raise the
  pretrade required-edge for that asset. Shadow-first when built.

## 3. Manipulation security (ADOPTED → TH-017 spoof flicker)

- Cartea–Jaimungal–Wang 2020 (spoofing operates through top-of-book
  imbalance; expected-fine is the only structural deterrent); Lee–Eom–
  Park (Korea Exchange: spoofers profit off imbalance-followers, pick
  thin/volatile/low-transparency targets, activity collapsed when depth
  disclosure changed); 2024 crypto L3 NN detector (posting distance is
  load-bearing; ~31% of large orders potentially spoof).
- Adopted: TH-017 — a level ≥ big_ratio× median top depth that vanishes
  ≥ drop_frac while the mid never crossed it (pulled, not consumed);
  per-side EWMA; entries in the baited direction shaded down. SAFETY
  shade: exempt from V2 reliability grading (a failed spoofer must not
  teach the ledger to ignore spoofing). Weekend books are exactly the
  thin/volatile venue class the Korea evidence flags.

## 4. Long-run growth math (validated our stack; one bound kept)

- Kelly via Breiman 1961 / Algoet–Cover 1988; MacLean–Thorp–Ziemba
  (fractional Kelly growth-security tradeoff; 2× Kelly → zero excess
  growth); Busseti–Ryu–Boyd 2016 (drawdown-constrained Kelly as convex
  program, dominates naive fractional Kelly); Moreira–Muir 2017
  (vol-managed portfolios) vs Cederburg et al. (not implementable
  out-of-sample; spanning weights are ex-post).
- Verdict: our stack already holds the survivors — fractional Kelly
  (0.25) + drawdown budgets + CVaR + vol scalar. The contested
  vol-timing ALPHA claim is NOT adopted (OF-4 discipline). Overbetting
  bound respected: kelly_fraction ≤ 0.5 stays a review tripwire.

## 5. Execution realism (designed, next build: queue-aware paper fills)

- Moallemi–Yuan (queue position value ≈ spread order); Huang–Lehalle–
  Rosenbaum 2015 queue-reactive model; verified: state-independent
  Poisson fill models OVERESTIMATE passive fill probability; honest
  public-data rule: an artificial resting order fills only when
  subsequent traded volume at its price exceeds the depth that was
  ahead of it at placement.
- Design accepted for the sim (deferred to next work block): track
  queue-ahead at placement from the book snapshot, drain it with
  observed trades at that level, fill on exhaustion. Kills the
  optimistic-fill bias in candidate labels. Caveat: queue-reactive
  formalism validated on large-tick assets; we adopt only the
  volume-drain accounting, not the full Markov model.
