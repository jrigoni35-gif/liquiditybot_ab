# Evidence-Closed Register

**Purpose.** [[synthesis/governance-doctrine|Governance rule 9]]: every rejection carries its
killing citation and **binds future sessions absent new evidence**. Before this page, those
rejections lived on four pages a fresh session might not open before proposing. This register
is the index — **check it before proposing any new technique, model layer, feature, or data
source.** The full adjudications live on the linked pages; this page never replaces them.

**The reopening rule** ([[concepts/evidence-grading-ladder]], durability rule — passed its
first live test 2026-08-08): a closed item reopens **only on its own stated revisit terms**,
never by re-litigation. The one recorded reversal is **US spot ETF flows** — rejected
2026-07-24 on availability, reversed 2026-08-08 when exactly the stated trigger (a usable
keyless source) fired. Everything else on this page has been re-killed or never retriggered.

## Techniques — closed

| Proposal (any variant) | Killed by | Full adjudication |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta-labeling / confidence filters / quality gates / abstention** | Theory: abstention cannot create edge where expected payoff is negative. Evidence: the only clean A/B in the literature (Wölner-Hanssen, Lund) found meta-labeling **worse on every axis**; zero independent empirical validation anywhere. | [[concepts/abstention-filters-ruled-out]] · [[sources/lund-meta-labeling]] |
| **Synthetic corpus data — SMOTE/oversampling, GAN/diffusion paths, jitter/augmentation, era transfer, relabeling** | Governance rule 11 (binding 2026-08-02): the corpus takes **nothing synthetic and nothing relabeled, ever**; the era-exclusion NO-ANALOGUE verdict is the measured ruled-out list. The corpus-corruption incident is what pooled relabeled rows DO. | [[synthesis/governance-doctrine]] rule 11 · [[concepts/era-exclusion]] · [[sources/session-20260809-corpus-corruption]] |
| **Exit-geometry rescue (a better bracket creates edge)** | The pre-registered 48-combo grid: **no surviving bracket** — exit design minimizes bleed, cannot create edge. | [[sources/session-20260802-digest]] · [[concepts/payoff-asymmetry]] |
| **Entry-timing signal claims** | Random-entry MFE control (n=51 vs 200 matched controls each): real entries' mean MFE percentile 0.516 [0.439, 0.594] — **no timing signal**. Caveat: n=51 rules out a LARGE edge only. | [[sources/session-20260802-digest]] · [[synthesis/owed-measurements]] item 0 |
| **"Corpus growth will fix the P&L" as a complete account** | Falsified 2026-08-09: gross P&L before any fees is −11.66 ≈ 0 — there is no gross edge to be starved of. (The DoF ledger's readings stand; the LICENCE to offer growth as the explanation is withdrawn.) | [[concepts/unfalsifiable-explanation]] · [[sources/session-20260809-unbiased-economics]] |

| **CapitolTrades / congressional STOCK Act PTR disclosures as a signal** *(added 2026-08-30)* | **Four independent kills, each sufficient alone.** (1) **CADENCE — closed twice already by two rows of this very register (see the `13F / N-PORT / Form PF` and `Equity-leads-crypto-by-HOURS` rows above):** the first closes the **45-day-lag cadence class** and congressional PTRs share that cadence; the second closes equity-leads-crypto because **documented transmission is MINUTES** while this feed is **30–45+ DAYS**. (3) **LEGAL, UNRESOLVED — [UNKNOWN], needs counsel, not an agent:** the Senate eFD gate quotes the **Ethics in Government Act** barring use *"for any commercial purpose other than by news media"*, **civil penalty up to $10,000**; whether systematic trading is such a purpose is not settled here. (4) **LITERATURE:** Belmont, Sacerdote, Sehgal & Van Hoek (2022), *J. Public Economics* **207:104602**, Jan 2012–Dec 2020 — **no evidence of superior performance**; even **95th/99th percentile** returns **consistent with random stock picking**. *Grade (4) honestly against the power arithmetic: it credibly excludes a LARGE effect and is underpowered for a small one — kills (1)–(3) do not depend on it.* Full adjudication: [[synthesis/public-record-data-atlas]] §5. **Revisit terms:** a cadence that is not 30–45+ days AND identified amounts AND a resolved legal read. Absent all three, do not re-propose. |

## Standing structural refusals (not evidence-closed — DESIGN-closed)

- **Deleting or rewriting learning data** — refused by design, machine-enforced
  ([[synthesis/governance-doctrine]] rule 8; mythos write policy BLOCKs `outputs/**`).
- **Injecting wiki/research content into the training corpus** — the flow is one-way
  (governance rule 12; corpus injection is deliberately GATED, ruled a feature).
- **Widening any gate to restore activity** — [[concepts/never-widen-a-gate]], with its two
  2026-08-09 boundaries (fail-closed disconnection is not widening; silent tightening is also
  forbidden).
- **Cohort-resetting changes during era-4 accrual** — the moratorium, governance rule 17:
  forbidden without operator adjudication because they mint the next
  [[synthesis/comparability-boundaries|comparability boundary]].
- **Model-side investment during the freeze** — frozen 2026-08-10; the ONLY unfreeze trigger
  is the era-4 gate readout at n≥50 ([[sources/session-20260810-stressor-epoch]] §1).
