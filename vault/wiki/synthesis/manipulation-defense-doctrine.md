# Manipulation-Defense Doctrine

## The convergence claim
The defense posture was built **twice, from two independent directions, and they agree.**

- **Bottom-up from microstructure** ([[sources/compounder-context-evidence]] §8,
  [[sources/research-20260718-sweep]]): spoofing profit estimates per unit intensity, wash-volume shares
  above 70% on unregulated venues, hundreds of documented pumps with ~65% spikes and full reversal,
  stop-cascade asymmetry.
- **Top-down from criminology** ([[sources/criminology-manipulation-lens]]): four theories of offender
  motivation, adjudicated on which explains the layer the bot actually transacts against.

**Both land on: veto-only, shadow-first, self-muting via a lower-bounded vindication ledger, with the
regulated execution venue as trust anchor.** The convergence is itself the load-bearing claim.

## The four pillars

### 1. Cost-imposition over detection
The only lever a single participant has over another actor's calculus is **zeroing the manipulator's
expected gain from you**. Distance-decayed order-book imbalance makes the cheap tactic (painting away
from the touch) worthless against this book specifically, pushing the adversary toward the expensive
at-touch variant. See [[concepts/cost-imposition]].

### 2. Veto rights, not alpha rights
**"A false shade costs opportunity; a false boost costs money."** Detectors shade down, never up; never
touch direction; never bypass a risk clamp. See [[concepts/asymmetry-law]].

### 3. Self-muting under an arms race
An adversary adapts, so a detector's edge decays. Weights scaled by a Wilson lower bound retire a stale
detector **automatically rather than by a fitted-literal edit** — which keeps the arms-race response
inside the no-fitted-literals discipline. Safety detectors are **permanently exempt** from
outcome-grading: "a failed spoofer must not teach the ledger to ignore spoofing." See
[[concepts/vindication-ledger]].

### 4. Exposure-limiting, not group-detecting
**The bot is a counterparty, not a surveillance system.** Social feeds are outside the observation
surface by design — "a deliberate scope boundary, not a gap." Building one would be **mandate creep with
no validation path**.

## The uncomfortable measurements
This doctrine is elegant and, as shipped, **largely inert**:
- Every shade event ever recorded was counterfactual — zero applied.
- The reliability layer had **never engaged and could not**, by construction.
- The live composite that *does* reach sizing shows **no dose-response** — the middle suspicion band
  performs worst, and the veto threshold has **never fired**.
- One mechanism, if enabled, would be a **provable no-op**.

See [[concepts/earning-its-keep-audit]] and [[comparisons/thales-engine-vs-manip-suspect]].

## The defensive corollary that is not about manipulators at all
Under an inferred-intent standard, the bot's own defense against being *labelled* a manipulator is a
complete reconstructable record. **"The answer is a file, not a recollection."** The instruction that
follows is unusual and worth preserving: **"protect it, don't extend it."**

## The owed test
Strain theory predicts cyclical manipulation pressure; rational choice predicts constant,
enforcement-gated pressure. **A stated testable divergence that nobody is scheduled to test.**
