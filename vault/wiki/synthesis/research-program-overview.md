# Research Program Overview

The master page. 35 sources spanning 2026-07-12 to 2026-08-01, covering architecture, assurance,
quantitative validation, and literature research for [[entities/liquiditybot]].

## The single most important structural observation
**Every document in this corpus runs the same adjudication grammar in a different domain:**

> grade the evidence -> name the encoding -> price the degrees of freedom -> record the killing
> citation -> make the rejection binding on future sessions

- Applied to **labeling**: [[sources/weekend-labels]] grades the pipeline against canonical pitfalls.
- Applied to an **external codebase**: [[sources/tradingagents-gap]] sorts into ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT /
  ALREADY AHEAD.
- Applied to **macro and psychology inputs**: [[sources/compounder-context-evidence]] grades 8 input
  families STRONG..REJECTED with a DoF cost each.
- Applied to **social theory**: [[sources/criminology-manipulation-lens]] scores four lenses and derives
  one actionable implication each.

**The grammar is the transferable asset, not the individual findings.**

## The four research arcs

### 1. The learning pipeline — see [[synthesis/learning-pipeline-arc]]
Label quality -> label era -> era exclusion -> deploy deadlock -> clock inversion -> horizon
re-alignment -> the discovery that the plumbing was never the binding constraint.

### 2. The money path — see [[synthesis/the-money-path-thesis]]
Every performance symptom reduces to one ratio: **cost/sigma = 0.82**.

### 3. Governance — see [[synthesis/governance-doctrine]]
Never widen a gate; measure before adopting; ship inert; prove inertness; shadow-first; price the
tuition.

### 4. Manipulation defense — see [[synthesis/manipulation-defense-doctrine]]
Derived twice, from microstructure and from criminology, converging on the same posture.

## Five program-wide invariants that survive every document
1. **Entries fail closed, exits fail safe.** The most-repeated rule in the corpus.
2. **[[concepts/asymmetry-law|Veto rights, not alpha rights]]** for every slow context layer.
3. **[[concepts/never-widen-a-gate]]** — shrink the space instead.
4. **Nothing is ever deleted** — corrections are reweightings and load-time views.
5. **Every disposition carries a registered code**, and the resulting audit trail is the intent record.

## What kind of program this is
> "A research program that ships almost nothing into the live path, deliberately."

Detection-only detectors, report-only instruments, shadow-mode adoption, DEFER verdicts, offline
digests. The corpus's dominant output is **measurement plus a documented decision not to act**.
