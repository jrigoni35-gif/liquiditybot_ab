# Governance Doctrine

The corpus's most transferable output. These rules are domain-independent.

> **Its companion page.** This doctrine governs **what may CHANGE**.
> **[[concepts/the-method]]** governs **what may be KNOWN** — the nine-stage
> investigation protocol every finding in this vault rests on, filed
> 2026-08-21 because until then it existed only as scattered practice across
> `USAGE.md`, the repo's `CLAUDE.md` and a dozen concept pages. Rules 14, 16,
> 18, 19, 20 and 21 below are the governance-side face of stages of that
> protocol; the method page is the place they compose into an order.

## 1. Never widen a gate
When a change fails an acceptance gate: revert, shrink the space, re-derive the mechanism, or file and
stop. **Relaxing the gate is never an option.** "Shrink the space, never the gate."

Operationalized: every ranked improvement lever carries an explicit **disqualifier** naming the
forbidden move. See [[concepts/never-widen-a-gate]].

## 2. Measured before eligible
No challenger becomes adoption-eligible until it has been **measured inside the validation machinery
under the rule the system actually runs** — never argmax. "Shipping the code path disabled is not, by
itself, admission." See [[concepts/gort-rule]].

## 3. Ship inert, and prove the inertness
New instruments ship with flags off, and their inertness is **proven, not asserted** —
md5-identical verdict lines, per-family retrain comparisons. See [[concepts/defer-verdict]].

## 4. Prove that drift is data, not code
Before re-baselining any metric, run the prior baseline commit against the current corpus in a scratch
worktree. Identical result means corpus-driven. See [[concepts/inertness-protocol]].

## 5. Shadow-first, promoted only on evidence
Every influencing mechanism starts non-influencing. **"Enabling detectors is not enabling influence."**
Promotion requires evidence beating a null, plus green batteries, plus a human. See
[[concepts/shadow-first-adoption]].

## 6. Price the tuition
Quantify what a learning intervention costs before adopting it. See [[concepts/priced-bleed]].

## 7. Name the mechanism before choosing the fix
**"No fix may be chosen until the mechanism is named."** When the system does not record the mechanism,
**the first deliverable is the recorder**. See [[concepts/iron-law-of-debugging]].

## 8. Nothing is ever deleted
Corrections are **reweightings** and **load-time views**. Every row stays byte-for-byte on disk. This is
what made the entire era saga reversible. See [[concepts/honest-data-framing]].

*Reaffirmed under direct challenge 2026-08-02 (follow-on session,
[[sources/session-20260802-digest]] third addendum):* asked whether any convicted/quarantined
records were recoverable or wrongly convicted, and whether deleting anything from the bot's data
or session memory would help profit — the measured answers were **none recoverable, none wrongly
convicted** (audit-crossref re-check of the quarantined rows: **0/136** live), **nothing
deletable would help profit**, and **deletion of learning data is refused by design**.

*Machine-enforced 2026-08-04* ([[sources/session-20260804-mythos-router]]): the
[[entities/mythos-router]] write policy (`.mythos/policy.json`) **BLOCKs** `outputs/**`,
`config.json`, and `.git` from all external write tooling — the audit chain, ledgers,
corpus, and guarded config are untouchable by machine policy, not just by doctrine.

## 9. Every rejection needs a killing citation, and binds future sessions
"Future sessions must not re-propose them without new evidence." **Unverifiability is disqualifying,
not an invitation to proceed cautiously.** See [[concepts/evidence-grading-ladder]].

## 10. Verify adversarially
Verifiers are instructed to **refute**. This is what catches fixes that are **correct in shape but inert
in effect**, and subsystems that pass every test while reaching no decision. See
[[concepts/adversarial-verification]] and [[concepts/earning-its-keep-audit]].

## 11. The corpus takes nothing synthetic
**Restated as binding 2026-08-02** ([[sources/session-20260802-digest]] second addendum):
research findings go to the wiki; the training corpus gets **nothing synthetic and nothing
relabeled, ever** — no augmented paths, no transferred-era rows, no relabeled history. Enforced
in practice by [[concepts/era-exclusion]]'s NO-ANALOGUE verdict and the measured ruled-out list
(GAN/diffusion paths, oversampling, augmentation).

## 12. The wiki is the truth
**Operator directive, issued verbatim 2026-08-02** ([[sources/session-20260802-digest]] final
micro-addendum):

> "From now on the corpus's Wiki is the truth and everything that is determined correct or has
> been determined correct needs to be injected into the corpus's wiki."

Standing doctrine, operationalized in three clauses:
- **Confirmed findings are filed same-session.** "Confirmed" means measured, battery-green, and
  committed. A finding that meets that bar and is not in the wiki is a governance defect, not a
  backlog item.
- **Hypotheses enter only as owed measurements** ([[synthesis/owed-measurements]]) — never as
  facts. The registers, not the prose, are where uncertainty lives.
- **The wiki is NOT the training corpus.** The flow is one-way: findings go to the wiki; rule 11
  still governs `signal_history` — nothing synthetic and nothing relabeled ever enters it.

*Applied as a rejection criterion 2026-08-04* ([[sources/session-20260804-mythos-router]]):
[[entities/mythos-router]]'s memory system was **REJECTed on this rule** — the vault is the
**sole brain**, and a third memory store would fragment truth. (The tool's own abandoned
scaffold, a `MEMORY.md` with zero entries, is the empirical exhibit of what an unmaintained
second store becomes.)

## 13. State the boundary — nothing here executes
**Operator directive, issued 2026-08-07** (verbatim intent on
[[concepts/paper-real-boundary]]): the bot executes **no real bids or positions** — every
fill, fee, queue, latency and markout is **simulated** — and this is **the central relation
of the project**, not a caveat: the corpus rehearses real-money discipline on simulated
execution. Operationalized:
- **Every conclusion filed states which side of the paper/real boundary its evidence comes
  from** (sim-side, venue-data-side, or repo-side).
- **Sim-conditioned numbers — fees, fills, markout, RTT, all dollar P&L — are never cited as
  venue truth.** The discipline transfers; the numbers do not.
- Boundaries *within* paper (the `8e5455e8` fill-regime boundary) are subordinate to this
  one and do not substitute for it.

## 14. Every house term carries its falsifier — an unbiased read never conforms to the bot
**Operator directive, issued 2026-08-09** (verbatim): *"make sure you're taking an unbiased
opinion... Not making those unbiased opinions (true data) conform to my bot"* — and the reason
it binds: *"this blocks the corpus from understanding real truth."* Filed as
[[concepts/unfalsifiable-explanation]].

This project's vocabulary — *cold-start*, *data-starved*, *exploration buys labels*, *tuition*,
*the learning curve is CLIMBING*, *DoF budget*, *era exclusion* — is individually defensible term
by term and, assembled, forms an account with **no failure state**: a losing week is tuition, a
failing gate is a corpus that needs to grow, a coin-flip model is a curve that has not plateaued.
Operationalized:

- **Before accepting any house-vocabulary account of a bad number, state what observation would
  FALSIFY it.** A term with no stated falsifier is rhetoric, not analysis.
- **No term is banned; every term owes its falsifier.** Two have already been tested and are
  recorded on their own pages: *"data-starved"* (**falsified as a complete account** — gross P&L
  before any fees is −11.66 ≈ 0, so there is no gross edge to be starved of) and *"exploration is
  tuition"* (**not yet** — OOF AUC 0.43–0.48 after 305 live labels).
- **A claim in the grammar of a measurement must have been measured.** `36fcfd6e`'s *"the
  identical red reproduces on the parent commit"* was asserted, never run, and carried the whole
  deploy argument ([[concepts/false-green]] design rule 8).
- **Prefer the cheap falsifier that already exists.** The decomposition that falsified the house
  account was one arithmetic identity over `state.json`, available for the project's entire life.

⚠️ **This rule was written because the wiki's own maintainer broke it twice in one session** —
once *into this wiki* ([[sources/session-20260808-night-staleness-overfit]], corrected in place),
attributing a corpus-corruption incident to "the cold-start corpus failing honestly, exactly as
the DoF adjudication predicts." Recorded here rather than buried, because a rule that pretends
its author never needed it is itself an explanation that cannot be wrong.
([[sources/session-20260809-unbiased-economics]])

## 15. A test double may only implement API the production object actually has
**Adopted 2026-08-09** from `1fee174e` ([[sources/session-20260809-adversarial-audits]] §1,
[[concepts/test-double-fidelity]]). The first rule in this doctrine aimed at the **test suite's
own premises** rather than at production code or at reasoning.

`risk/position_sizer.py` read `getattr(state, "positions", {})` at three sites. **`PortfolioState`
has no `positions` attribute** — the book is `_positions`, exposed as `open_positions()` — so the
**default was taken unconditionally for the life of the module**, three risk controls were inert,
**all three failed permissive**, and tickets ran **13.7% larger than designed**. **~3,400 tests
were green over it**, because the doubles supplied the attribute production lacks.

- **A double's surface is an unpinned claim about production.** `class _State: positions = {...}`
  asserts that production states have `.positions`. Nothing checked it.
- **Defensive `getattr` against your own types is the smell.** It converts the loudest, cheapest
  possible signal — `AttributeError` — into a silent default. Prefer the access that crashes.
- **Derive doubles from the production accessor**, never from a hand-written attribute set.
- **The rule is two-sided:** a double must implement the production API **and only** the
  production API. The corpus already holds the opposite-sign specimen — a fixture that predated
  an instrument and so caused a test to **decline to protect a real metric**
  ([[concepts/iron-law-of-debugging]], the inverted hazard).
- **Corollary, and it is the expensive one:** *a test whose docstring names a bug class is not
  coverage of that class.* The specimen's docstring described this exact failure mode and its
  fixture reintroduced it one level up ([[synthesis/documentation-drift-register]]).

> **Why it belongs in governance rather than in a style guide.** Rule 1 says never widen a gate.
> A too-generous double **widens every gate that runs behind it, silently, with no diff to
> review** — and it does so in the flattering direction, because the fabricated object is always
> the well-behaved one.

## 16. A disposition gets the same adversarial pass as the finding it closes
**Adopted 2026-08-10** from the resolution of [[synthesis/open-contradictions-register]] entry
25 — the fill-sim double-count, correctly named on 08-07, was **talked away at the DISPOSITION
step on 08-08** ("conservative floor, by design") and confirmed right on 08-10 at **1.88x**.
The measurement never changed; **the finding was lost at adjudication**, in a session careful
enough to catch its own test-count error.

- **A disposition that closes a docket item deserves the same adversarial pass as the finding
  that opened it** ([[concepts/adversarial-verification]]) — **and MORE when written beside a
  fix the author is pleased with.**
- The [[concepts/self-flattery-gradient]] acts on **adjudications, not only measurements**:
  the comfortable disposition ("by design," "conservative," "already handled") is the one to
  attack hardest.
- A closure whose only evidence is the closer's own framing is a **claim**, not a closure —
  rule 14's grammar test applies to dispositions verbatim.

## Sibling doctrine: risk posture
This page governs **how change is allowed to happen**. The 2026-08-02 operator directive on
**why the bot trades at all** — rent-money vigilance on one side, mandatory calculated risk on
the other — is filed separately as [[synthesis/risk-posture-doctrine]]. The two meet at rule 1:
deployment pressure is never answered by loosening a floor.

## The meta-rule
**DEFER is a successful outcome.** "A DEFER verdict is a successful, informative outcome of this
measurement exercise — not a failure of it; nothing here was stretched to manufacture an ADOPT."

## The failure mode this doctrine does not prevent
It is superb at preventing *bad changes* and poor at forcing *the right question*. The system ran
disciplined measurement for two weeks while the binding constraint — cost/sigma — went unmeasured
because **no gate asked about it**. Rigor about how you change things is not rigor about whether you are
changing the right thing. See [[synthesis/the-money-path-thesis]].

**Confirmed again, and worse, 2026-08-09** ([[sources/session-20260809-unbiased-economics]]).
The same failure mode ran for **another week** at a larger scale: **gross P&L before any fees —
the single number that says whether the strategy has any edge at all — had never been computed**,
over ~250 closed positions and 438 entry fills, and it turned out to be **−11.66 (≈0)** against
**382.59** of fees. No gate asked; **and the vocabulary supplied an answer that made the question
feel already-settled**, which is the aggravating factor rule 14 exists to address.

> **The sharpened statement.** The doctrine's blind spot is not merely that *no gate asks the
> economic question* — it is that **a fluent internal vocabulary can make the unasked question
> feel answered.** Rule 14 is the first rule on this page aimed at the corpus's own reasoning
> rather than at its code.

## RULE 18 — Stay current, and never leave an orphan claim (2026-08-14)

**Operator directive.** Full statement on [[concepts/no-orphan-claims]]; schema copies in the
vault's `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` as domain rule 10.

> The wiki is not a place knowledge is **stored**. It is the place knowledge is **kept true**.

Three obligations, in order, on every question:

1. **Consult first.** `wiki/index.md` before deriving anything. *(Measured cost of skipping:
   a 1.12M-token architecture fan-out re-derived a taxonomy this vault already held. The spend
   was not the error — the order was.)*
2. **When the wiki does not know, reference back.** Answer from the primary artifact the page
   cites — `raw/`, a commit, a `file:line`, a ledger, a measurement — never from recall, and
   cite what you walked to. A silent page is an instruction to go one level down, not evidence
   of absence. **This runs against the vault's own content**: recall-before-derive must never
   become reuse of a cached conclusion as evidence.
3. **When no reference exists, find one.** *"No citation available" is a task, not a
   disposition.* An unsourced claim is not filed and not acted on; it becomes an entry in
   [[synthesis/owed-measurements]], a graded pass on [[concepts/evidence-grading-ladder]], or
   a measurement run **now** — never a fact.

**Currency is part of correctness.** A page a measurement contradicts gets its callout in the
**same session**, both sides (rule 6). Deferring is not a smaller version of correcting:
between the measurement and the edit the page is actively wrong and is being read as settled.
This is rule 12 (*file confirmed findings same-session*) applied to the negative case —
**filing what is newly true and un-filing what is newly false are one obligation.**

**Why this rule is on the reasoning side, like rule 14.** Rules 1–13 and 15–17 govern what may
change in the *code*. Rules 14 and 18 govern what may be *believed*. Rule 14 said every house
term carries its falsifier; rule 18 says every claim carries its source, and that obtaining a
missing source is work to be done rather than a conclusion to be reported.

**Its own directive session supplied the type specimen** — recorded per rule 16, since a rule
filed without its own failing case is the kind of claim it forbids: that session reported owed
67's ALGO-5 trigger as fired "6x over" at 182 uncensored paths. It had not fired. Item 67's
measure is the ALGO-4 ledger (`outputs/trade_paths.csv`, **10 rows** against ~30); the 182 were
`signal_history` **barrier resolutions**, a different population whose name also reduces to
"paths". It was caught only because the ingest checklist forced the owed page open — i.e. by
obligation 2 being **mechanically enforced** rather than remembered.
([[sources/session-20260814-cohort-instruments]])

## RULE 19 — A session is not a citation (2026-08-16)

**Operator directive** — *"everything — even the fact sessions can get mixed up — needs to
be apparent to the wiki."* Full statement on [[concepts/session-identity-is-not-stable]];
measured in [[sources/session-20260811-16-vscode-3b307393]] §1.

> A session is a **process**, not an **observer**. It has no reliable clock, no guaranteed
> continuity with its own subprocesses, and no notice that the world moved while it was
> suspended.

**Measured:** one VS Code session ran **four calendar days** across several host restarts with
its in-context "now" frozen at **2026-08-12** while disk reached **2026-08-16** and **33
commits** (24 first-parent) from other sessions landed unseen. Five failure modes were observed
in that one session: a frozen clock; background monitors dead with the host while the session
still awaited their notifications; MCP servers detaching mid-conversation; a user interrupt
killing four investigator subagents and leaving a run with **no completion record** (its
journal: 8 `started`, 4 `result`); stale, out-of-order notifications.

**Three obligations:**

1. **Every filed claim carries a session identity AND a wall-clock date.** A session id alone is
   ambiguous across four days of its own life.
2. **A conclusion carried inside a long-lived session is RECALL, not evidence** — re-derive it
   against disk before acting. This is rule 18's obligation (b) and the delegated-measurement
   contract's *re-derive, don't recall*, now with a **carrier-level mechanism** rather than a
   caution about model memory.
3. **Assume every pre-gap background task is dead.** Before acting, re-derive wall clock, git
   branch + tip, and bot vitals (`status.json` age, `runner.lock` age, `pythonw` count) — the
   **wake protocol**, snapshot-stamped, never recalled.

**Its own directive session supplied three type specimens** (recorded per rule 16): "25
commits" (**33** / 24 first-parent), "the contamination outlived the fix by ~21 h"
(**24.74 h**), and "the journal showed 4 started and 0 result" (**8 started, 4 result**). Each
was a correct statement about a world that had moved — with the citation formatting intact,
which is precisely rule 18's most dangerous failure mode.

## RULE 20 — An unstated protocol is an absent protocol (2026-08-16)

**Operator directive**, same day. **Full statement, and the durable home of the law itself:
[[concepts/iron-law-of-repair]]** (added 2026-08-16). Delegation half on
[[concepts/location-not-magnitude]] §the protocol does not travel;
[[sources/session-20260811-16-vscode-3b307393]] §5.

**Subagents do not inherit the parent's skills.** Therefore every delegated agent that **may
EDIT anything** carries the focused-fix five-phase protocol embedded **BY PATH** in its prompt
(`C:\Users\haird\.claude\skills\focused-fix\SKILL.md`): **SCOPE → TRACE → DIAGNOSE → FIX →
VERIFY**, IRON LAW *no fixes without completing scope/trace/diagnose first*, **escalate at 3+
cascading fixes**.

Same failure shape as the delegated-measurement contract, whose cause is measured: under-
determined specs produced **5 of 9 wrong numbers** in one session because **agents fill gaps
silently rather than halting**. Pair rule 20 with rule 19's re-derivation duty and with
*ship the pointer, never the number*.

> [!warning] **OPERATOR CARVE-OUT — STATUS: TEMPORARY, ACTIVE 2026-08-16. NOT STANDING LAW.**
> **AMENDED 2026-08-16, same session as its filing.** The carve-out was first filed here as
> standing rule text; the operator has since said it is **"just temporary"**. A temporary
> suspension filed as standing law is exactly the stale-page failure rule 18's currency clause
> exists to prevent — everything in this vault reads as settled. **The DEFAULT state of rule 20
> is the full Iron Law.** This is a live suspension that **EXPIRES rather than accrues**: a later
> session may **not** cite it as settled practice, and if it is load-bearing it must be
> **re-confirmed with the operator first**. Full text and expiry rule:
> [[concepts/iron-law-of-repair]] §the carve-out.

**What is exempt is the PHASE GATE. Rigor is never suspended — only ceremony.** Optimizations
skip the five phases; they do not skip: **read the module first** (no guessed APIs, no assumed
signatures, no invented attributes — the ~3,400-green-tests-over-a-fabricated-object specimen,
[[concepts/false-green]] §the sixth way) · **measure rather than infer** (an optimization's claim
IS its before/after number) · no silent behavior changes · no cheating the code to move a number ·
the DoD matrix still runs. Named precedent: **parallelizing the Kraken book fetches measured ZERO
gain and was reverted — the rate limit was the wall, so the lever was cutting call COUNT, not
concurrency.**

*Same shape as rule 1:* **speed is licensed, assumption is not** ≡ *a gate may be DISCONNECTED but
never WIDENED* ([[concepts/never-widen-a-gate]]) — a structural relaxation is permitted only while
the measurement that actually protects the system is untouched.

**Scope note:** rules 19 and 20 govern *how work is carried and delegated*, not what may change
in the code. Neither relaxes rule 17 — a delegated agent running the five phases is still
forbidden from cohort-resetting changes without operator adjudication.

## RULE 21 — A claim page declares its status, or it is read as SETTLED (2026-08-21)

**Operator directive.** Full statement, the three states and the enforcement:
**[[concepts/claim-status-discipline]]**. Schema copy in the vault's `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md`
as domain rule 12. This is stage 9 of **[[concepts/the-method]]**.

> **A wiki has no neutral register. FILING SOMETHING IS THE CLAIM THAT IT IS SETTLED** — that
> is what a reader takes from the act of it being written down, dated, cross-linked and
> indexed. So an in-flight finding filed without a marker does not read as "preliminary". It
> reads as **true**. An unmarked in-progress finding is not incomplete; it is **misinformation,
> believed because it is written down.**

**Every claim page declares `status: SETTLED | PROVISIONAL | SUPERSEDED` in frontmatter, and
the two non-settled states declare it again VISIBLY in the body** — Obsidian's reading view
does not render frontmatter, so a status that lives only in the YAML is invisible to the human
the rule exists to protect.

- **SETTLED** — measured, the measurement ran, **and its refuters ran and failed**. A claim
  about the *evidence*, not about the future. **It may not be stamped in bulk**: stamping a
  page SETTLED because nobody objected is this vault's own orphan-claim failure at scale.
- **PROVISIONAL / WIP** — the investigation is open. **Actionable as a LEAD; never citable as
  evidence; nothing downstream may depend on it.** Must carry a visible callout, a
  `## What would settle this` section (*a provisional claim with no settle-condition never
  settles — it ages into being believed*), and its **PENDING REFUTERS** named, default
  disposition **REFUTED**. It also gets an [[synthesis/owed-measurements]] entry — a
  provisional page and an owed measurement are one object seen from two directions.
- **SUPERSEDED** — overturned, never deleted (rule 8). Must route the reader forward by
  wikilink; a superseded page with no supersedor is a dead end that still reads as an answer.

**ENFORCED, NOT ADOPTED** ([[concepts/adoption-is-not-enforcement]]):
`~/.claude/skills/llm-wiki/scripts/lint_claim_status.py` fails the vault on an invalid value, a
PROVISIONAL page missing its callout / settle-condition / refuters, or a SUPERSEDED page with
no supersedor — **mutation-verified 2026-08-21, 6/6 checks fired on their planted defect with a
clean control and a clean restore.** Ledger pages (index, log, and the four registers) are
exempt by name because every entry already carries its own disposition. What the linter cannot
see is stated on its own page: **a page that is wrong-but-declared-SETTLED passes it and always
will.**

**Where it sits among the rules it extends.** Rule 12 says *what to file*; rule 21 says **what
filing asserts**. Rule 18 says a page a measurement contradicts gets its callout the same
session — rule 21 covers the case rule 18 cannot: **the measurement has not finished yet.**
Between "no finding" and "a contradicted page" sits a real state that had no vocabulary. And
rule 20's carve-out is this rule's direct ancestor: a *temporary* suspension was filed as
standing rule text and had to be amended within the same session, **because everything in this
vault reads as settled.** Rule 21 generalizes that one amendment into a required field.

**Its own type specimen, recorded per rule 16.** [[synthesis/live-readiness-verdict]] was filed
hours earlier the same day as a final-reading five-blocker VERDICT while a cost-stack
investigation reframing its central economics was still running
([[sources/session-20260821-cost-stack-in-flight]]: mean gross measured **POSITIVE +0.0733%**
over 434 closed positions against a **0.717%** configured fee stack — fees ~10x the gross edge,
~16x at the true Tier-1 40/80). **Nothing on that page was false.** It simply could not say
*"an open investigation may move this."* The verdict page is now PROVISIONAL; its NOT-READY
direction and all five blockers are unaffected. **The failure was produced by a session
following every other rule correctly** — which is the argument for a required field rather than
a remembered habit.

**Retroactive scope, and the deliberate limit.** Applied to the six pages of the 2026-08-21
session plus that session's new pages. **Not** applied to the other **203** (final count 212 scanned — 7 SETTLED, 2 PROVISIONAL, 0 errors), which remain
**UNDECLARED** — an honest third reading meaning *no session has asserted a status here*.
Bulk-stamping 203 pages SETTLED would be a 203-page orphan claim filed in the name of a rule
against orphan claims. Declaring page-by-page as each is next touched is a **task** on
[[synthesis/owed-measurements]], not a disposition.
