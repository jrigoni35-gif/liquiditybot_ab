### 10b. NEW — the gate's SE uses a POPULATION denominator, and the bias is flattering

Found by the correction pass while double-deriving the sd, **not reported by either
agent**. `cohort_eval.py`'s `gross_se_pct` is **`pstdev / sqrt(n)`**, not
`stdev / sqrt(n)` — the **biased, n-denominator** estimator:

| estimator | sd (gross) | SE at n=16 | SE at n=50 (`n_eff` 16.405) | 2·SE floor at n=50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **population — as shipped** | 2.559127919795581% | **0.6397819799488953%** | **0.6318323922199418%** | **1.2636647844398836%** |
| sample / unbiased | 2.6430559504487245% | 0.6607639876121811% | 0.6525536887099274% | **1.3051073774198547%** |
| **bias** | **+3.2795558988644613%** | | | **floor understated by 3.28%** |

`0.6397819799488953 × 4 = 2.5591279197955812` = `pstdev` **exactly**, which pins the
denominator without reading further code.

**Why it matters and why it is small:** the direction is **flattering** — a smaller
SE makes the resolution floor look **nearer** and an effect look **more resolvable
than it is**, which is the [[concepts/self-flattery-gradient]] signature this vault
tracks. At n=16 the bias is 3.28%; at n=50 it would fall to ~1%. It does **not**
change owed 82's conclusion (the floor is still **1.92×** the observed mean, and
correcting it moves that to **1.98×** — *worse*, not better). Registered as **owed
82(b)** rather than a new item, because it is a property of the same instrument and
the same signature line. **Not a defect to "fix" reflexively** — the era-4
registration is a measurement standard, and changing an estimator mid-accrual is
the shape [[concepts/never-widen-a-gate]] forbids. It must be **named in the
readout**, not silently patched.
