# Wölner-Hanssen — *Evaluating the Effect of Meta-Labeling on Equity Market Neutral Strategy*

Lund University student thesis (LUP Student Papers record 9120301, open-access PDF). The `raw/`
snapshot is the LUP record page; ingested 2026-08-02 ([[sources/session-20260802-digest]] second
addendum).

## Why it is in this vault
It is the **only clean A/B design** in the meta-labeling evidence base: the same primary strategy
run with and without the meta-labeling layer, compared on like terms. Result: **meta-labeling was
worse than no meta-labeling on every axis.**

This makes it primary evidence **FOR** the standing rejection in
[[concepts/abstention-filters-ruled-out]] — the rejection's strongest empirical leg now has its
source snapshot in `raw/`, satisfying the rule that every rejection carries its killing citation
([[concepts/evidence-grading-ladder]]).

## Evidence-grade caveats
- A **student thesis**, not peer-reviewed journal work — it belongs to the six-thesis independent
  base described in the rejection page, and is cited *as* that, not as more.
- Equity market neutral, not crypto market-making; the transfer argument runs through the theory
  leg (abstention cannot create edge where expected payoff is negative), which is domain-general.
