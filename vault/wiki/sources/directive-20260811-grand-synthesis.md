## Execution plan, sequenced INTO the standing laws

**PHASE A — synthesis at full scale** (ultracode workflow). Hold **ALL** open issues in one
frame — owed 52–64, the queue items, every minor residual — and analyze **interactions, not
just fixes**: past effects (the *measured* cost of each defect), present (live stressor state
under the $800 regime), future (cohort/boundary/learnability impact of each candidate change).
This is the directive's "put them together like a human would" — the docket has only ever been
adjudicated item-by-item.

**PHASE B — algorithm design.** Candidate mechanisms from the engineering sweep, filtered by
the academic sweep's established/contested/folklore grades
([[concepts/evidence-grading-ladder]]), **parameterized ONLY from the bot's own uncensored
data** (`outputs/trade_paths.csv` — no borrowed constants, no tuned-to-tape magic numbers),
validated against the quant gates (G1–G5, OF-1..7) **before touching config**. The
[[synthesis/evidence-closed-register]] is checked before any candidate is proposed — closed
items stay closed.

**PHASE C — introduction in dependency order.** One **adjudicated geometry-epoch package** —
cheapest at low accrual, currently **0/50** on the era-4 cohort — then accrual, then the gate
readout. The geometry-epoch **timing adjudication is PENDING with the operator**; when minted
it will be **cut #7** of [[synthesis/comparability-boundaries]]. Model-frozen items remain
frozen **until the gate speaks**.

**PHASE D — "teach it to want this for itself."** Mechanized honestly as
[[concepts/closed-loop-self-measurement]]: the bot has no wants; the buildable equivalent is
that **every new algorithm ships WITH its own self-measurement**, so the system generates the
evidence for its next escalation or its own stop. See the concept page for the three loops that
already exist (RP-072 ladder, postmortem→monitor, era-4 gate) and what the synthesis adds.

## The honesty line (file with it, quote with it)

**Nothing here decrees profit.** The sequencing maximizes **P(finding edge if it exists)** and
**speed-of-knowing if it does not**. The era-4 gate **remains sole arbiter** — NO_GROSS_EDGE
routes to the operator's stop-strategy question, not to another round of tuning. Any "path of
profit" claim beyond this is the [[concepts/self-flattery-gradient]] operating on a directive —
the most dangerous place for it, because a directive's framing propagates into every session
that executes it.

## Also recorded: the complete trade-path ledger (the directive's data prerequisite)

**The censoring it closes** ([[concepts/uncounted-exclusion]], the winners-censoring instance):
`postmortem_summary.csv` records **only EV-underperformers**, so the excursion paths of WINNING
trades — the MAE envelope of trades that paid, the one distribution any **evidence-derived stop
geometry** needs — were computed by `_excursions` and then **discarded** (the same
computed-and-thrown-away class as the drawdown gauge). **The winners' heat profile did not
exist on disk**: 15 positive-realized rows in 266 summary rows at ship time (**re-verified on
the box at filing: 269 rows, 15 positive** — accrual moved between ship and filing).

**The mechanism** (verified in the working tree): `ml/postmortem.py` `PATHS_COLS` (line 72,
16 columns: ts, position_id, asset, direction, p_win, expected_pct, realized_pct, mfe_pct,
mae_pct, held_h, stopped_out, recovered_after_stop, stress_during_hold, regime_entry,
regime_exit, cause) → `_write_path` appends **one row per finalized close, winners included**
(`poll()` line 288 — unconditional, outside the underperformer trigger), via `durable_append`
(the torn-append gate), exception-guarded so a lost telemetry row never breaks the close.
**`cause` is EMPTY for a trade that performed to expectation — named, not omitted**, so the
censoring stays visible in the schema itself. **Summary semantics byte-untouched** (a winner
still creates no `postmortem_summary.csv`). **3 tests** (`tests/test_trade_paths.py`): winner
reaches paths but not summary; underperformer reaches both with identical MAE; column pin plus
the point of the whole ledger — a winner that dipped −1.5% before paying keeps its adverse
excursion on disk. Moratorium-SAFE: measurement only, nothing about which orders are placed or
how they fill.
