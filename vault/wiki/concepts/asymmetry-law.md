# The Asymmetry Law

## Statement
> **"A false shade costs opportunity; a false boost costs money. Slow layers get veto rights, not alpha
> rights."**

Any context layer whose cadence is too slow to ever accumulate an evidence certificate within the
lifetime of a decision — yearly trends, macro regimes, geopolitical context — **may only shade DOWN**.

## Where it binds across the program
- Macro/structural inputs are "**structural-stress confirmation**, never a signal generator, gate, or
  sizer input."
- Sentiment is "a filter, never a trigger," enforced *structurally* by limiting the scanner's consumers.
- SMC and THALES feature columns "never touch `direction` or `all_confirmed`."
- Halving-phase context may only **tighten** (tighten the ratchet, slow the add cadence), never boost.
- Where volatility evidence is real but direction evidence contradicts, the only admissible encoding is
  a **cadence pause**, never a signed direction.
- Detector weights **attenuate only** — "a proven detector approaches but never exceeds its configured
  gain."

## The mechanical justification
Supplied independently from criminology: the only lever a single market participant has over another
actor's cost-benefit calculus is **zeroing the manipulator's expected gain from you** — defense, not a
counter-bet. See [[concepts/cost-imposition]].
