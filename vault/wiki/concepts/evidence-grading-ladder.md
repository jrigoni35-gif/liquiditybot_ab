# Evidence Grading Ladder

## The ladder
**STRONG / MODERATE-STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK / REJECTED**, applied per candidate input family and
**separately for *existence* versus *direction***.

## Why the existence/direction split matters
Several inputs have real, replicated evidence that *something happens* and contradictory evidence about
*which way*. The admissible encoding for those is a **cadence pause, never a signed direction** — the
volatility evidence is real, so slow down; the direction evidence contradicts, so do not bet.

## The killing citation
**Every REJECT must name the specific paper, contradiction, or absence that killed it.** Examples:
- A calendar effect killed by a later study showing the effect diminished and one sign **flipped**.
- A macro input killed **by absence** — no peer-reviewed study exists, and the observed correlation is
  already captured by an existing feature.
- A strategy killed by its own headline: a reported Sharpe fit in-sample over three cycles with no
  purge, null, PBO or deflation is **"a red flag, not a benchmark."**

## Two non-evidence disqualifiers
- **[[concepts/availability-failure-mode]]** — MODERATE evidence plus no keyless machine-readable
  source equals rejected regardless of evidence quality.
- **Cadence kill** — a series too slow for the decision cadence is a dead feature by construction.

## Durability
Rejections become part of the record and bind future sessions absent new evidence. This is what stops a
knowledge base from re-litigating the same eight candidate inputs every quarter — and it is the single
most wiki-native rule in the corpus.

**The rule passed its first live test on 2026-08-08**
([[sources/session-20260808-institutional-data-adjudication]]): the ETF-flows availability
rejection was **reversed on exactly its stated revisit terms** (published study + usable
source — the world changed, so the parked rejection un-parked), while the candidates that
came back around (put/call as direction, IV-skew as direction, stablecoin issuance) were
**re-killed with fresh citations rather than re-litigated**, and thirteen new kills entered
the record each carrying its killing citation. The ladder also gained a sharp instance of
the existence/direction split: perp **funding's existence evidence is a model feature
already, but its adopted direction encoding is an asymmetric veto only** — extreme positive
funding truncates longs, never signs an entry ([[concepts/asymmetry-law]]).

## Second full-scale application (2026-08-10): the Grand Synthesis academic sweep
The ladder ran again at scale on the stops/turns/carry/labels literature
([[sources/sweep-20260811-academic-stops]]), this time with a three-way surface grading —
**ESTABLISHED / CONTESTED / FOLKLORE** — layered on the ladder's grammar. Notable ladder
mechanics in that filing: an established result carrying its own live contest in the same row
(Moreira-Muir, contested by the Cederburg 103-strategy replication — both halves filed, nothing
adopted on it); a folklore grade that names the one legitimate residue inside the folklore
(MAE-boundary placement — tautological as a rule, legitimate as a diagnostic); and a graded
**GAP** — a question the literature cannot answer at all (triple-barrier label shift across
regimes), which converts into local measurement
([[synthesis/grand-synthesis-algorithm-package|ALGO-2]]) rather than into a borrowed claim.
The sweep's grades directly filtered the engineering candidates
([[sources/sweep-20260811-engineering-precedents]]) before anything reached the package.

## Honesty convention
Unverifiable claims are flagged as such in place — author lists not confirmed, journal publication not
confirmed, canonical-from-knowledge rather than search-verified. **Unverifiability is disqualifying
rather than an invitation to proceed cautiously.** *(Applied at the 2026-08-10 sweep filing: the
underlying agent report was not accessible to the wiki session, so every citation is flagged
as-relayed on the sweep page itself, with recovery of the report as the stated revisit term.)*
