# The Paper/Real Boundary (nothing here executes)

## The directive

Issued by the operator 2026-08-07, filed same-session under
[[synthesis/governance-doctrine|governance rule 12]]. Verbatim intent:

> "Make sure the Wiki knows it is not executing bids/positions itself but it is a big relation
> and datapoint that needs to be apparent in all of the corpus's mindset considering that is
> the point."

Two clauses, both binding. **(1) The fact:** the bot executes **no real bids and holds no real
positions** — `system.dry_run = True`, `outputs/force_dry.on` present, invariant 1 of
[[entities/liquiditybot]] (dry-run defaults true; live requires a config change, a restart, and a
typed ceremony phrase). **(2) The mindset:** this is not a footnote to be attached to dollar
figures — it is **the central relation of the project**. The corpus exists to rehearse
real-money trading discipline on simulated execution. That is *the point*: the value produced
here is the discipline, the mechanisms, the defect classes, and the honest measurement habits —
not the paper P&L. A paper book run casually would rehearse nothing.

## What is real

The **mechanism layer** is fully real, which is why incidents here are evidence: the decision
pipeline, order flow logic, gates, audit chain, reason codes, restarts, persistence, the live
Kraken **market data** (public WS books and trades), the churn loops, the code paths that fired
and the code paths that silently didn't. The 147-lap hedge churn was a **real control-loop
failure**; its **-$297 was simulated**. Both halves of that sentence must always travel together.

## The filing rule (binding, corpus-wide)

1. **Every conclusion filed states which side of the boundary its evidence comes from.**
   "Measured" alone is not enough — measured *on what*? A fill-count is sim-side; a WS book
   snapshot is venue-side; an audit-chain property is repo-side (real).
2. **Sim-conditioned numbers are never cited as venue truth.** Fees, fill rates, queue outcomes,
   markout, RTT, and every dollar of P&L are conditioned on the simulator's assumptions. The
   panel's type specimen: the ETH/BTC "quoter edge" (+14.8/+11.2 bps maker markout) was **sim
   physics**, not edge ([[sources/session-20260807-institutional-review]] §f).
3. **Dollar figures carry their pricing assumption.** The drawdown −384.67 is a **floor** if a
   live account would sit at Kraken Tier 1 (40/80) and an overstatement at Tier 5 (~15/30);
   under a falsified fee constant it is a *conditional* number either way.

   (`8e5455e8`, `passive_base_prob` 0.45 → 0.048 — [[synthesis/risk-posture-doctrine]]) made the

## Why this is the point, not a limitation

[[synthesis/risk-posture-doctrine]] orders the bot to trade "as if real rent-money is on the
line at all times." That directive only makes sense **because** no real money is on the line:
the posture is a deliberate rehearsal, and the rehearsal is only worth running if the paper
environment is never allowed to flatter (paper may only err *against* the strategy —
[[concepts/cost-truth]]) and never allowed to be **mistaken for the performance**. The
institutional review's recurring failure shape — a sim artifact read as a market fact — is
exactly the failure this page exists to make impossible to file.

> **The bot is a flight simulator that logs like an airline.** The logging discipline is real
> and transfers to live one-for-one. The flight never left the ground, and no conclusion about
> lift may be filed without saying so.
