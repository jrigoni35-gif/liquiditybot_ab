# Four Criminology Lenses Compared

| Lens | Mechanism | Bot-side match | Persistence forecast |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Differential association** | manipulation is *learned* in coordinated groups | **outside the observation surface by design** | persists and **adapts** regardless of countermeasures |
| **Rational choice** | offend when expected gain exceeds detection x penalty | **densest — every mechanism it names has a file:line** | **enforcement-gated, roughly constant** |
| **Strain** | goal/means gap and acute loss drive high-variance behavior | **not observable in market data, "and nothing should try to"** | **loss-cycle-gated, cyclical** |
| **Labelling** | social reaction constitutes deviance; stigma is reclaimed | reflexive only — protects *us* from being mislabelled | fades only on a cultural shift |

## Why rational choice wins
It explains the layer the bot actually transacts against tick-by-tick (algorithmic spoofing and
layering), and it is the only lens whose predicted mechanism the system can act on: **push the
manipulator from the cheap tactic to the expensive one**. See [[concepts/cost-imposition]].

## What each contributes that the others cannot
- **DAT** explains the *retail* pump layer's persistence and adaptation — and justifies the scope
  boundary rather than a new detector.
- **ST** is a background-conditions theory explaining the **supply** of participants, and it delivers
  the observation that the bot is **structurally strain-immune by construction** (dry-run default,
  typed arming ceremony, risk stack) — "the exact opposite of what strain theory predicts a strained
  human trader reaches for."
- **LT** explains why the retail layer resists self-policing, and yields the one defensive
  implication: under an inferred-intent standard the audit trail is the defense.

## The discriminating experiment nobody is running
**Strain predicts cyclicality; rational choice predicts constancy.** Explicitly called "a testable
divergence" with no experiment proposed. See [[concepts/persistence-curve]].

## The honest asymmetry
The document argues theory choice matters operationally, then returns **four "nothing actionable"
verdicts**. Its real product is confirmation of the existing posture plus one untested prediction.
